About Me

Hello! I am Tehreen Naz, a DPT (Doctor of Physical Therapy) undergraduate student. Alongside my studies, I teach school students and help them understand their subjects in a simple, organized and encouraging way.

My main teaching subject is Mathematics. I also teach English, Urdu, Biology, Social Studies and Islamiat according to the relevant class syllabus.
